# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

The project is an educational C command-line repository. Its issues, pull requests, discussions, and contribution channels should remain welcoming, respectful, technically useful, and safe for people with different experience levels.

This policy applies whenever a person participates in project spaces or represents the project.

## 2. Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique code, behavior, and technical proposals rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Give beginners room to ask questions without ridicule.
- Accept good-faith corrections and maintain technical discussions on topic.
- Respect privacy and avoid exposing personal details or credentials.
- Credit original work and identify third-party material.
- Follow the repository contribution and security-reporting procedures.

## 3. Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, repeated unwanted contact, sexualized attention without consent, deliberate disruption, personal insults, malicious impersonation, retaliation for reporting concerns, and publishing another person's private information.

Spam, knowingly false accusations, and deliberate attempts to derail technical discussion may also be treated as violations.

## 4. Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers should:
- Review conduct reports as promptly as practical.
- Protect reporter privacy where possible.
- Separate technical disagreement from personal misconduct.
- Preserve relevant context needed to assess a report.
- Apply proportionate and consistent enforcement.
- Explain the outcome to involved parties when it is safe and appropriate.

A report is not automatically proof of a violation. The maintainer should consider the available context before deciding on action.

## 5. Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through the contact method available on the @Brajesh31 GitHub profile. Include the relevant repository location, approximate date, participants involved, and links or evidence where safe.

Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public issue. For a security vulnerability, use the private reporting procedure in SECURITY.md instead.

If a concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's platform-level reporting and moderation tools. Do not assume this repository can independently investigate platform-wide conduct.

## 6. Investigation process

The maintainer may request clarification, review public repository records, remove harmful content, pause a discussion, or restrict participation while assessing a serious concern. Confidential information should be shared only with people who need it to handle the report.

The project does not promise a fixed investigation deadline or a formal appeal service. A reporter may provide additional relevant information if the initial decision missed material context.

## 7. Enforcement

Depending on severity and pattern, consequences may include:
1. A private or public warning.
2. Removal or editing request for harmful content.
3. Temporary moderation or participation restrictions.
4. Temporary suspension from project spaces.
5. Permanent exclusion from project spaces.
6. Escalation through GitHub's platform reporting tools where appropriate.

The maintainer should consider impact, intent, repetition, and prior conduct. Retaliation for a good-faith report is itself unacceptable.

## 8. Scope and limitations

This policy governs project-managed participation spaces. GitHub platform rules and policies also apply. The policy is not a substitute for emergency services, legal advice, or a guaranteed independent dispute-resolution process.

## 9. Related policies

See CONTRIBUTING.md for technical contribution rules, SECURITY.md for vulnerability disclosure, and GOVERNANCE.md for project decision authority.
